## POSITION

**The LAST content pane on the sidebar, directly above SETTINGS.**

## PURPOSE

The player-facing **customization + tuning** surface for the device family.

## LAYOUT

**Mirrors the SERVERS pane grammar** — the same three-part split (**Hub / Terminal / GPU Racks**) — but
**every card routes to customize-and-tune, not status.** (Servers shows state; Customize changes it.)

## CONTENTS (per Bloodwave)

1. **Cosmetic upgrades** — the Cosmetic Firewall surface (donor QoL; **Law 17 immune** per the
   terminal doctrine cosmetics lane).
2. **Progress display.**
3. **Donation perk ACTIVATIONS** — Donor Law holds: **social / QoL only, zero power.**
4. **Device TUNERS, sold by role:**
   - **"Overclock upgrading"** — **RISKY tier, sold by the BLACK MARKET DEALER** (fits the
     capability-keystone pattern).
   - **Cooling tunes + other buffs** — sold by the **TECHNICIAN.**
5. **`$LP` purchases** — e.g. **2× payout multipliers** (donor-multiplier ruling lineage, `fable\0068`).
6. **TEMPORARY BUFF ACTIVATIONS** — anticipated; **this pane owns their activation UI** (timers,
   active-effect list).

## ECONOMY GUARD (standing law, carried verbatim — not new)

**Items 4–6 touch payout math.** Every tuner / buff / multiplier **lands through the same ledger
discipline as lpbitcoin** — **no new faucet class.** The **2×-payout via `$LP`** intersects
`DUAL_CURRENCY_IDENTITY_RULING_2026-07-15` and Donor Law:

> ⚠ **OPEN RULING — donor-multiplier vs zero-power boundary.** Whether **paid payout multipliers are
> QoL or power** needs **one Bloodwave ruling BEFORE build.** Flagged, **not blocking design.** (Donor
> Law's "zero power" line meets a paid mechanic that changes payout rate — the boundary is a ruling,
> not a UI call.)
