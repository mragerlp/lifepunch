# Compliance

LifePunch work on DXRP must remain compatible with:

- DXRP terms and portal rules.
- S&box platform rules.
- Facepunch platform policies.
- Steam account/subscriber rules.

Observed DXRP Terms page:

- URL: `https://dxrp.net/terms`
- Last updated: April 2026.
- DXRP describes itself as a roleplay platform built on top of S&box by Facepunch Studios.
- Terms apply to the web portal, in-game systems, APIs, and associated services.

## Operating Rule

Review compliance when work touches:

- Public addons.
- Server automation.
- API integrations.
- Website integrations with DXRP data.
- Discord bots/webhooks tied to moderation or player data.
- Economy-affecting systems.
- Staff permissions and sanctions.

Do not treat screenshots as the full legal source. If a decision depends on terms or policy, review the live terms page directly.
