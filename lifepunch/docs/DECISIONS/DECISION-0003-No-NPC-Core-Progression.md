# DECISION-0003 — No LIFEPUNCH™ NPC dependency

> **Status:** Active  
> **Date:** 2026-06-25  
> **Proposed by:** Design Architect  
> **Approved by:** Bloodwave  
> **Supersedes:** —  
> **Law:** `LIFEPUNCH_GAMEPLAY_LAWS.md` G9

## Decision

LIFEPUNCH™ systems must **not introduce, require, or depend on NPCs**. Gameplay is player-driven and machine/infrastructure-driven.

Third-party NPC functionality that may exist elsewhere in DXRP is outside LIFEPUNCH™ design scope and must never become a LIFEPUNCH™ gameplay dependency.

## Reason

Players operate real infrastructure (machines, economy, PvP) — not quest givers or static vendors. Aligns with industrial operator fantasy and DXRP roleplay without binding LIFEPUNCH™ design to NPC systems.

## Alternatives considered

- NPC shopkeeper for upgrades — **rejected**
- NPC tutorial miner — **rejected**
- Optional LIFEPUNCH™ NPC flavor later — **rejected** (product direction is no NPC dependency)

## Systems affected

All future addons · Bitcoin · Banking · Hacker (when unlocked)
