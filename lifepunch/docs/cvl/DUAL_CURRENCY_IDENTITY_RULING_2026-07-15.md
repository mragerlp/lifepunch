## 1. THE TWO CURRENCIES ARE DIFFERENT BY DESIGN

| Currency | Model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **`$LP`** | **PORTAL-PERSISTENT.** Carried on the portal, so **donations have clear received / used digital-goods records.** | `$LP` is the durable, account-level currency. Its portal persistence is what makes purchase/donation auditable as digital goods. |
| **`$BTC`** | **SESSION-BASED BY DESIGN** (portal-backable **later**, when `$LP` wiring lands). | It is **more valuable session-based** — the in-game **grind drives skill-tree upgrades**, and the hub provides a **cash-out before logoff** path **without owning a miner**. Persistence would flatten the grind's value. |

**This resolves `BRAND_DOCTRINE §4`:** the website selling `$LP` is **not** a contradiction — `$LP`
**is** the portal-persistent currency, purchasable with auditable digital-goods records; the
**play-earned grind is `$BTC`** (session). The two answer different halves of the economy. The
`$LP` **sign/colour** (blue `#017AEF` PENDING-RATIFICATION) is a **separate open question** and is
**NOT** resolved here.

**Store-tab shape (was gated on this):** the Store surface prices **portal-persistent `$LP`** as a
digital-goods/monetisation surface with clean records; `$BTC` is the session grind, cashed out at the
hub. Donor Law still governs what `$LP` may buy (convenience/expression, never combat power).
