## 1. THE MARK IS SCOPED TO THE PLAYER HUB

**The LP roundel and all LIFEPUNCH brand marks appear on the PLAYER HUB ONLY.**

`BRAND_DOCTRINE.md §1` names the official mark (the blue LP roundel,
`lifepunch/legal/marks/lifepunch-logo_source_1006.png`). This ruling scopes **where it is
allowed to render**:

| Surface | Mark in header |
|---|---|
| **Player Hub** (identity home) | **The LP roundel / LIFEPUNCH marks.** This is the one place the brand identity lives in-game. |
| **Job / system UIs** — Bitcoin Ops (HASHD), future Chemist, and **every other job surface** | **Their OWN purpose-built logo/icon.** Bitcoin Ops carries the **HASHD** mark. **The LP roundel is OUT of job surfaces.** |

**Why:** the Player Hub is the player's identity surface; job UIs are *tools*. A job screen wearing
the house brand blurs the line between "this is LIFEPUNCH" and "this is the thing you use inside
LIFEPUNCH." Each job owns its mark; the house owns the hub.

## 2. UNIVERSAL HEADER + FOOTER STRUCTURE, PER-MENU ACCENT

**The proprietary header/footer is the ONE structure across every menu. The structure never
changes; only the accent colors do.**

- **STRUCTURE = UNIVERSAL.** The existing proprietary footer/header style is the single frame
  every LIFEPUNCH menu wears — Player Hub, Bitcoin Ops, future job menus. Same anatomy everywhere.
- **ACCENT = PER-MENU.** Each menu adopts the accent colors of **its own** UI styling (the hub's
  `$lp-blue` wash; Bitcoin Ops' HASHD amber; the next job's own accent). The frame is constant;
  the color inside it is the menu's own.
- **The structure itself never changes.** A menu does not restyle the frame — it recolors within it.

### ⚠ NO R7 CONFLICT

**R7 removed the DXRP-style footer. This footer is OURS.** The universal frame ruled here is the
LIFEPUNCH proprietary header/footer, not the DXRP-native footer/watermark that R7 struck and that
`LIFEPUNCH_UI_STANDARD` Law 8 bans. They are different objects: R7/Law 8 kill the *inherited DXRP
chrome*; this ruling standardizes *our own frame*. A seat reading Law 8's "no footer in-HUD" must
read it with `MENU_LAW`/this ruling's supersession note (see `LIFEPUNCH_UI_STANDARD.md`, Law 8
note) — the ban is on borrowed chrome, not on the proprietary LIFEPUNCH frame.
