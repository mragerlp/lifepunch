## CATEGORY 1: SERVERWIDE RULES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. English Only  
   - English speaking community. RP in English.

2. No Cheating  
   - 3rd party software, cheats, macros, or autoclickers result in a PERMANENT ban.

   - Map, item, or tool exploits for unfair advantage. (Excludes drug creation/gunshops).  
   - You may not base outside of the map. The rocks are the barrier.

4. No Mic Spam  
   - Purposely being disruptive to RP.

5. No Staff Impersonation  
   - Results in a PERMANENT ban.

6. Do Not Lie to Staff  
   - False reporting or deleting ticket evidence is punishable.

7. No Staff Baiting  
   - Saying you will break a rule = breaking a rule.

8. No Minimodding  
   - Don't threaten reports. Report properly and move on.

9. Admin Final Say  
   - Do not argue with staff about rules & punishments.

10. No Begging  
    - No solicitation of real money or IRL items.

11. No Bullying  
    - Targeting/harassing outside of RP is never tolerated.

12. No Politics/War/Religion  
    - No political arguments. Jokes okay, arguments not.

13. No Racism/Homophobia  
    - Zero tolerance. Results in PERMANENT ban.

14. No Sexual/NSFW Content  
    - Media must stay PG. Includes ERP/Porn.

15. No Doxing  
    - Posting IRL pictures without permission is a PERMANENT ban.

16. Cybercrime Threats  
    - DDoS or Doxing threats result in a PERMANENT ban.

17. No Advertising  
    - Only official DXRP or S&box links allowed.

18. No IRL Illegal Activity  
    - Encouraging illegal activity = Permanent ban.

❗ Staff always have final say in situations not listed!

## CATEGORY 2: BASIC RP RULES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

### SUB-CATEGORY 2A: RDM / RDA
──────────────────────────
1. RDM Logic  
   - No killing/arresting without valid RP reason. Disrespect is not a reason; a threat is.

2. RDM KOS Line  
   - KOS zones must start at a bases front door (map or fading)  
   - Crossing a KOS sign/line is NOT RDM. KOS line must be at the main entrance/, but applies to the entire base.

3. RDM Warnings  
   - You may kill after warning someone 3 times in chat to step away.

4. RDM Police  
   - You may be killed by the person you're trying to arrest.

5. RDM Mayor  
   - Killing the Mayor requires a valid RP (PD raid, mug, kidnap/hostage).

### SUB-CATEGORY 2B: NLR (New Life Rule)
────────────────────────────────────
1. NLR Trigger  
   - Applies on death, job change, and jail release (unless escaped).

2. NLR Past  
   - You may remember past events, but cannot act on them.

3. NLR Raid  
   - You may not return to a raid after dying for 10-minutes.

4. NLR Revive  
   - Revived players may continue their raid/scenario.

5. NLR Hitman  
   - Hitmen cannot re-attempt failed hits (your hit fails after death).

### SUB-CATEGORY 2C: Fail RP
────────────────────────────────────
1. Fail RP Logic  
   - Actions that break character, violate the established setting's logic, or disregard server rules, resulting in poor-quality, unrealistic, or disruptive play.

2. Examples:  
   - Stealing your base mates valuables and then starting a new base.  
   - Mugging someone together, and then killing your partner.  
   - Police working with Thieves or Gangsters.  
   - Door camping/blocking doors.  
   - Merchants cannot scam.

────────────────────────────────────

6. Merchant Deny

2. Demote Reasons

4. Scamming

## CATEGORY 4: BUILDING RULES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

### SUB-CATEGORY 4A: PROP & WIRE
─────────────────────────────
1. Spawn Build  
   - No building in spawn. No prop climbing, flying, or blocking.

2. Wire Abuse  
   - No Wire abuse (auto-stealing money, loud sounds, stealing shipments).

3. Prop Permission  
   - No props in other players' property without permission.

### SUB-CATEGORY 4B: FADING DOORS
───────────────────────────────────
1. Fading Door Limit  
   - Max 2 fading doors to get access to your raidables.  
   - ex. *one-way exits, peeking holes, merchant airlocks*

2. Fading Door Utility  
   - Utility doors (one-way exits, peeks) are allowed.

3. Fading Door Airlocks  
   - Airlocks must be identifiable and distinct (color/material).

### SUB-CATEGORY 4C: BASE LAYOUT & FAIRNESS
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Base Reachable  
   - Bases must be reachable/accessible at all times.

2. Base Entrance  
   - Min/Max 1 entrance. Unused map doors must be blocked.

3. Base Jump/Crouch  
   - Raiders must never be forced to crouch or jump at any time inside/outside OR to gain access to a base.

4. Base Mazes  
   - No Mazes.  
   - A maze is defined as: more than 1× 180° turn OR 2× 90° turns OR multiple disorienting/excessive pathways used to artificially extend raid duration.  
   - Artificially built raid hallway's / airlocks must not exceed 25 1x1 (1000 units) excluding natural map layouts.

5. Base KOS Line  
   - KOS zones must start at a bases purchasable front/fading door or at the start of an airlock.  
   - KOS lines must have a text sign that says KOS past.

6. Base Shooting  
   - Raiders must be able to clearly see you and shoot back.  
   - Cannot use tiny hitboxes for unfair advantage (Considered Malicious).

7. Base Crowbar  
   - Bases must be crowbar-raidable (no code-only).

8. Base Damage  
   - Bases cannot damage players.

9. Base Movement  
   - No slowing/impeding movement.

10. Base Entrances  
   - Entrances must be reasonably easy to find.  
   - Be visible & distinct from surrounding walls/terrain (color/ material).  
	   - This includes no-collide area's  
   - Entrance minimum 2x2 standing area (80x80 units).

11. Base Walkways  
   - Walkways must be at least 1x1 prop (40 units) wide.  
   - This includes ramps.

12. Base No Collide  
   - No collided props must not be confusing for raiders.  
   - Example: No collided props must be a different color/material.

13. Entity Ladders  
   - Bases must not require the use of entity ladders at any time.

### SUB-CATEGORY 4D: PLACEMENT & MAP RULES
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Public Space  
   - Do not take up excessive public space.

2. Other's Property  
   - Do not build into other people’s property.

3. PD Building  
   - Do not build in PD if not Government.

4. Blocking Off  
   - Do not block weed drop-off, ATMs, trash cans, recycler.

5. Drop-Offs  
   - Weed drop-off must be fully walkable.

### SUB-CATEGORY 4E: SPECIAL BUILDING RESTRICTIONS
───────────────────────────────────

1. Only buy doors you intend to use.

2. No skybases or excessive aerial builds.

3. Max 1 connection to a drug drop-off location.

4. Decorative aerial builds allowed.

5. No blackout bases.

6. KOS zones must be understandable and never deceptive.

## CATEGORY 5: JOB SPECIFIC RULES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

### SUB-CATEGORY 5A: MAYOR & CP
────────────────────────────
1. Mayor  
   - Must base in PD.   
   - Gun licenses: can charge fee, not obligated to provide to criminals.   
   - Can build outside only for government or RP use (checkpoints/toll booths).  
   - Announce major law changes before enforcing.

2. Police  
   - Must base in PD.   
   - Must allow all members of Government to Base/RP with you.  
   - Follow hierarchy: Mayor > Chief > Officer.  
   - Must attempt to arrest before killing (unless weapon present)  
   - Can build outside only for government or RP use (checkpoints/toll booths).

3. Laws  
   - Must be reasonable.  
   - Not contradict server rules (making RDM allowed).  
   - No text-only disrespect laws.  
   - AoS laws are allowed, such as j-walking, guns out, etc.  
   - KoS laws are NOT allowed.  
   - Laws cannot target specific individuals or jobs (ex. Hobo's are illegal).

4. Arrests  
   - Only for lawbreakers.  
   - Cannot arrest innocents (even if bribed).

5. Lockdowns  
   - Lockdowns: Outdoors only. You may arrest, not KOS. Valid reason needed (Bank raid/shooting).

6. Corruption  
   - Allowed in RP.  
   - Not allowed against other government members.  
   - Bribes are okay. Helping criminals raid PD or killing Government is forbidden.  
   - You may NOT: Kill the mayor/officers, let criminals into restricted areas, place hits against Government.

7. Warrants  
   - Require valid RP evidence. Must SEE illegal activity.  
   - No metagaming to warrant (hearing printers/drugs/discussions).   
   - Expire on death, jail, or successful raid defense.

8. Checkpoints and Tolls  
   - Must not block any spawn-area entrance/exits  
   - Must not extend raid-durations  
   - Tolls cannot exceed $50  
   - 2 Checkpoints/ Toll-booths MAX

9. Searches  
   - Searches require RP reason  
   - Examples: gunshots nearby, loitering near drug drop

### SUB-CATEGORY 5B: CIVILLIAN
────────────────────────────────────
1. Gun Dealer  
   - Must intend to sell.  
   - Cannot base with another gun dealer.  
   - Can defend a criminal base.  
   - Must sell individual weapons (not only shipments).

2. Medic  
   - Only 1 per raid party.

3. Theatre Manager  
   - Must base in the Theatre.

### SUB-CATEGORY 5C: CRIMINAL
────────────────────────────────────
1. Hitman  
   - No metagaming hits.  
   - Only Hitman can perform hits.  
   - Doesn't need a valid RP reason unless it is the Mayor.  
   - Hitman may only raid active target locations max one Hitman per raid.
