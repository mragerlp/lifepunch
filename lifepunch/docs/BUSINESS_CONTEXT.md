## Revenue model (what the business does / how it earns)

- **Does:** custom LIFEPUNCH-owned content for DXRP (s&box) — addons/weapons/entities, staff/admin
  tooling, server/community operations; licensing that content to other DXRP servers.
- **Earns:** digital products/addons, server/community, donations, and (potential) licensing —
  all under LIFEPUNCH as the single source. Original LIFEPUNCH content is proprietary IP (no resale/
  redistribution by others; anti-clone + DMCA via the website TOS §5–6).

## Financing the AI build (if pursued)

If financing **Cornerman** (the local AI workstation) or related infra, frame it accurately as
**software-development equipment** for the LLC's documented dev activities, backed by the business
profile (LLC, EIN, business bank account, `lifepunch.co`, records of revenue/donations).

> ⚠️ Accuracy note vs. generic loan advice (`BUSINESSMODEL.txt`, 2026-06): that guidance references
> "RTX GPUs / dual-GPU AI servers" and an ~"$8,000 workstation." **Our actual hardware is the Corsair
> AI Workstation 300 — AMD Ryzen AI Max 385 / Radeon 8050S iGPU (unified memory, ~48 GB as VRAM), not
> NVIDIA RTX.** Use the real spec and real cost when representing the purchase. The generic loan-type
> overview (SBA, line of credit, equipment financing, revenue-based) is fine as background only.
