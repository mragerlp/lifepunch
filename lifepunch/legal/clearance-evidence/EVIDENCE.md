# Clearance Evidence — lifepunch.net common-law user (dormancy)

Captured **2026-06-09** to preserve evidence that the senior common-law user of "LifePunch"
(the Garry's Mod community at `lifepunch.net`) appears **dormant / non-using**. Relevant if that
community (or a successor) ever opposes or petitions to cancel the LIFEPUNCH registration:
**3+ consecutive years of non-use is *prima facie* abandonment** under the Lanham Act, and abandoned
marks cannot support a priority/likelihood-of-confusion challenge.

See the clearance summary in `../TRADEMARK_AND_IP.md` §6.

## Why this matters

- The `lifepunch.net` community is the only material clearance watch-item (a senior, ~2011 user of a
  near-identical name in a related gaming field).
- Their public-facing activity stopped **~May 2020**, and the site origin is currently **offline (522)**.
- They are also self-described **non-commercial** ("There is no premium store. We don't want your
  money."), which weakens any protectable *commercial* trademark rights.
- Together these support an **abandonment** position and reduce the practical risk of a successful
  challenge to the LIFEPUNCH filing.
