no word text (the word LIFEPUNCH is covered separately by wordmark #402997).

## Decision: COLOR CLAIM (selected 2026-06-09)
Filing the logo **in color, claiming blue** — use `lifepunch-logo_drawing_color_900.jpg`. This locks
the blue as a feature of the mark. (Trade-off accepted: narrower than a no-color-claim filing, which
would have covered the shape in any color.)

A color claim requires two extra statements on the application — provide both verbatim:
- **Color claim statement:**
  > The color blue is claimed as a feature of the mark.
- **Description of the mark:**
  > The mark consists of a blue circle containing a stylized "LP" monogram (the letters "L" and "P")
  > appearing in white negative space within the circle. The color white represents background and/or
  > negative space and is not claimed as a feature of the mark.
