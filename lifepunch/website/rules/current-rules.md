# LifePunch Official Rules Snapshot

Source: `https://lifepunch.co/rules`

This is a public rules snapshot for review and website maintenance. Direct website links should use stable rule anchors where possible.

## Serverwide Rules

- English Only: English speaking community. RP in English.
- No Cheating: 3rd party software, cheats, macros, or autoclickers result in a permanent ban.
- No Exploiting: Map, item, or tool exploits for unfair advantage are forbidden. This excludes drug creation/gunshops. You may not base outside of the map. The rocks are the barrier.
- No Mic Spam: Purposely being disruptive to RP.
- No Staff Impersonation: Results in a permanent ban.
- Do Not Lie to Staff: False reporting or deleting ticket evidence is punishable.
- No Staff Baiting: Saying you will break a rule equals breaking a rule.
- No Minimodding: Do not threaten reports. Report properly and move on.
- Admin Final Say: Do not argue with staff about rules and punishments.
- No Begging: No solicitation of real money or IRL items.
- No Bullying: Targeting or harassing outside of RP is never tolerated.
- No Politics/War/Religion: No political arguments. Jokes are okay; arguments are not.
- No Racism/Homophobia: Zero tolerance. Results in permanent ban.
- No Sexual/NSFW Content: Media must stay PG. Includes ERP/Porn.
- No Doxing: Posting IRL pictures without permission is a permanent ban.
- Cybercrime Threats: DDoS or doxing threats result in a permanent ban.
- No IRL Illegal Activity: Encouraging illegal activity results in permanent ban.
- Staff always have final say in situations not listed.

## Basic RP Rules

### RDM / RDA

- RDM Logic: No killing or arresting without valid RP reason. Disrespect is not a reason; a threat is.
- RDM KOS Line: Crossing a clearly marked KOS line is not RDM.
- RDM Warnings: You may kill after warning someone 3 times in chat to step away.
- RDM Police: You may be killed by the person you are trying to arrest if the situation escalates.
- RDM Mayor: Killing the Mayor requires a valid RP reason, such as PD raid, mug, kidnap, or hostage.

### NLR

- NLR Trigger: Applies on death, job change, and jail release unless escaped.
- NLR Past: You may remember past events, but cannot act on them.
- NLR Raid: You may not return to a raid after death.
- NLR Revive: Revived players may continue their raid/scenario.
- NLR Hitman: Hitmen cannot re-attempt failed hits. A failed hit cannot be retried after death.

### Fail RP

- Fail RP Logic: Actions that break character, violate the established setting's logic, or disregard server rules, resulting in poor-quality, unrealistic, or disruptive play. Examples include stealing base mate valuables and starting a new base; mugging then killing your partner; police colluding with criminals; door camping/blocking; merchant scamming.

## Common Sense And Behavior

### Basic Guidelines

- FearRP: Not enforced, but value your life reasonably.
- Government Raid: Government cannot raid with criminals except Hitman.
- No Suicide RP: No suicide to avoid RP scenarios such as kidnaps or mugs.
- Job Arrest: Government cannot raid or arrest just because of someone's job.
- Job Description: Must follow job descriptions. Medics heal, Merchants/Dealers sell, Cops protect.
- Merchant Deny: Denying Merchant service for non-RP reasons is forbidden, such as denying gun sales to a potential raider.

### Conduct Rules

- No Job Change: No changing jobs during active RP.
- Demote Reasons: AFK 30 minutes or more, not doing job, police corruption, scamming.
- No Vigilante: Do not punish rulebreakers yourself with AOS, KOS, or propblock.
- Scamming: Merchants cannot scam.

## Building Rules

### Prop And Wire

- Spawn Build: No building in spawn. No prop climbing, flying, or blocking.
- Wire Abuse: No Wire abuse, including auto-stealing money, loud sounds, or stealing shipments.
- Prop Permission: No props in other players' property without permission.

### Fading Doors

- Fading Door Limit: Max 2 fading doors to get access to your raidables.
- Fading Door Utility: Utility doors such as one-way exits or peeks are allowed.
- Fading Door Airlocks: Airlocks must be identifiable and distinct by color/material.

### Base Layout And Fairness

- Base Reachable: Bases must be reachable and accessible at all times.
- Base Entrance: Minimum and maximum of 1 entrance. Unused map doors must be blocked.
- Base Jump/Crouch: Raiders must never be forced to crouch or jump at any time inside/outside or to gain access to a base.
- Base Mazes: No mazes. A maze is more than one 180-degree turn, two 90-degree turns, or multiple disorienting/excessive pathways used to artificially extend raid duration. Artificial raid hallways/airlocks start at your KOS sign and must not exceed 25 total 1x1 props or 1000 units, excluding natural map layouts.
- Base KOS Line: KOS zones must start at a base's purchasable front/fading door or at the start of an airlock. KOS lines must have a text sign that says `KOS past`.
- Base Shooting: Raiders must be able to clearly see you and shoot back. Tiny hitboxes for unfair advantage are not allowed.
- Base Crowbar: Bases must be crowbar-raidable. No code-only bases.
- Base Damage: Bases cannot damage players.
- Base Movement: Bases cannot slow or impede movement.
- Base Entrances: Entrances must be reasonably easy to find, visible, and distinct from surrounding walls. Minimum 2x2 standing area, 80x80 units.
- Base Walkways: Walkways must be at least 1x1 prop, 40 units wide, including ramps.
- Base No Collide: No-collide props must not be confusing for raiders and should be visually distinct.
- Entity Ladders: Bases must not require use of entity ladders at any time.

### Placement And Map Rules

- Public Space: Do not take up excessive public space.
- Other's Property: Do not build into other people's property.
- PD Building: Do not build in PD if not Government.
- Blocking Off: Do not block weed drop-off, ATMs, trash cans, recycler.
- Drop-Offs: Weed drop-off must be fully walkable.

### Special Building Restrictions

- Only buy doors you intend to use.
- No skybases or excessive aerial builds.
- Max 1 connection to a drug drop-off location.
- Decorative aerial builds allowed.
- No blackout bases.
- KOS zones must be understandable and never deceptive.

## Job Specific Rules

### Mayor And CP

- Mayor: Must base in PD. Gun licenses can charge a fee and are not obligated for criminals. Can build outside only for government or RP use, such as checkpoints or toll booths. Announce major law changes before enforcing.
- Police: Must base in PD. Must allow all members of Government to base/RP with you. Follow hierarchy. Attempt to arrest before killing unless weapon present.
- Laws: Must be reasonable. Must not contradict server rules. No text-only disrespect laws. AOS laws allowed; KOS laws not allowed. Laws cannot target specific individuals or jobs.
- Arrests: Only for lawbreakers. Cannot arrest innocents, even if bribed.
- Lockdowns: Outdoors only. You may arrest, not KOS. Valid reason needed, such as bank raid or shooting.
- Corruption: Allowed in RP. Not allowed against other government members. Bribes are okay. Helping criminals raid PD or killing Government is forbidden.
- Warrants: Require valid RP evidence. Must see illegal activity. No metagaming. Expire on death, jail, or successful raid defense.
- Checkpoints and Tolls: Must not block any spawn-area entrance/exits. Must not extend raid durations. Tolls cannot exceed $50. Two checkpoints/toll booths maximum.
- Searches: Require RP reason, such as gunshots nearby or loitering near drug drop.

### Civilian

- Gun Dealer: Must intend to sell. Cannot base with another gun dealer. Can defend a criminal base. Must sell individual weapons, not only shipments.
- Medic: Only 1 per raid party.
- Theatre Manager: Must base in the Theatre.

### Criminal

- Hitman: No metagaming hits. Only Hitman can perform hits. Does not need a valid RP reason unless target is the Mayor. Hitman may only raid active target locations. Max one Hitman per raid.

## Raiding And Mugging

### Basic Raid Rules

- Raid Guidelines: Raid starts when prybar is out, base member is damaged, weapon is out on property, or refusal to leave property occurs. Raid ends when no raiders remain inside or on property.
- Mid Raid: No props can be moved, changed, deleted, or added during raids. You can use an entity ladder to get into a flawed/open base.
- PD Raid: Raid ends when all PD attending raid die. Returning to a PD raid after death breaks NLR.

### Job Guide

- Citizen: cannot raid.
- Cook: cannot raid.
- Gun Dealer: cannot raid.
- Medic: can raid.
- Hitman: can raid.
- Government: can raid.
- Hobo: can raid.
- Thief: can raid.
- Gangster: can raid.
- Mob Boss: can raid.
- Government: cannot mug.

### Mugging Rules

- Mugging Limit: Max mug is $1,000. Must type warning, not voice only. 10-second response time required.
- Mugging Cooldown: 5-minute cooldown per different target; 10-minute cooldown for the same person.
- Mugging Defense: A victim of mugging/kidnapping is always allowed to defend themselves without warning.
- Mugging Shipments: Can mug shipments/guns if you see someone collect it; same warning rules apply.

## Cooldowns And Reporting

### Cooldowns

- Mugging different people: 5 minutes.
- Mugging same person: 15 minutes.
- Hits same person: 10 minutes.
- Raiding same person: 30 minutes.
- PD Raid: 10 minutes.
- Mayor: 10-minute grace before they can be raided or killed.
- Mayor Kidnap: 20 minutes.
- No raiding for 10 minutes after server crash.

### Reporting Rules

- Report Respect: Be respectful. Do not spam reports. Provide proof such as Medal, OBS, or Steam.
- Report Lying: Lying to staff results in a permanent ban.
- Report Use: Use `@` or `/Staff` in-game. EVIPs can jail in severe cases.
