## Serverwide Rules

- English Only: We're an English-speaking community. Please keep all roleplay in English.
- No Cheating: Using third-party software, cheats, macros, or autoclickers will result in a permanent ban.
- No Exploiting: Exploiting maps, items, or tools for an unfair advantage is not allowed (drug creation and gun shops are excluded).
- No Mic or Text Spam: Intentionally disrupting roleplay through your microphone or chat spam is not allowed.
- No Staff Impersonation: Impersonating staff will result in a permanent ban.
- Do Not Lie to Staff: False reporting or deleting ticket evidence is punishable.
- No Staff Baiting: Saying you will break a rule counts as breaking that rule.
- No Minimodding: Do not threaten others with reports. Submit reports properly and move on.
- Admin Final Say: Do not argue with staff about rules or punishments. Staff always have the final say in situations not listed.
- No Begging: Soliciting real money or real-life items is not allowed.
- No Bullying: Targeting or harassing players outside of roleplay is never tolerated.
- No Politics/War/Religion: Political arguments are not allowed. Jokes are fine; arguments are not.
- No Racism/Homophobia: There is zero tolerance for racism or homophobia. Violations will result in a permanent ban.
- No Sexual/NSFW Content: All media must remain PG-rated. This includes ERP and pornographic content.
- No Doxing: Posting real-life pictures of someone without permission will result in a permanent ban.
- Cybercrime Threats: Threatening DDoS attacks or doxing will result in a permanent ban.
- No Advertising: Only official DXRP or S&box links are allowed.
- No IRL Illegal Activity: Encouraging illegal real-life activity will result in a permanent ban.

## Basic RP Rules

### 🔫 RDM / RDA

- RDM Definition: Random Deathmatch (RDM) is killing someone without a valid roleplay reason.
- RDA Definition: Random Death Arrest (RDA) is arresting someone without a valid roleplay reason.
- RDM Reasoning: Disrespect or threats alone are not valid reasons to kill someone.; Taking damage or having items stolen are valid reasons.
- Warnings: You may kill someone after warning them three times in chat to step away or to leave your property.
- Police: You may be killed by the person you are trying to arrest or their basemates.
- Mayor: Killing the Mayor requires a valid roleplay reason, such as a PD raid or mugging.

### ⚠️ KOS

- KOS & RDM: Crossing a clearly marked KOS line is not considered RDM.
- KOS Line Placement: KOS zones must start at a base's purchasable front door, fading door, or the start of an airlock.; KOS lines must include a text sign that says 'KOS past.'; Government jobs cannot have KOS lines.
- KOS Boundaries: KOS lines are general markers for where KOS begins for the inside of a base.; Once a KOS line is placed, the space behind that line is considered KOS.; Example: If a KOS sign is at the front door, but you enter the base through a window, you may still be killed.
- KOS Clarity: KOS zones must be easy to understand and must never be deceptive.

### 💀 NLR

- NLR Definition: The New Life Rule (NLR) means you may remember past events, but you cannot act on them.
- NLR Trigger: NLR applies on death, job change, and jail release (unless you escaped).
- Raid: You may not return to a raid after death.; You must wait until the raid is completed to return to your base as a defender.
- Revives: Revived players may continue their raid or scenario.
- Hitman: A failed hit cannot be re-attempted.; A hit fails upon death.

### 🎭 Fail RP

- Fail RP Definition: Fail RP is roleplay that breaks character, violates the setting's logic, or disregards server rules, resulting in poor-quality, unrealistic, or disruptive play.
- Examples: Stealing your base mate's valuables and then starting a new base; Mugging someone with a partner, then killing that partner; Police working with thieves or gangsters; Door camping or blocking doors

### 🔍 Metagaming

- Metagaming Definition: Metagaming is using out-of-character (OOC) knowledge to influence your character's in-game decisions.; It occurs when your character acts on information they realistically should not know, bridging the gap between what you know and what your character knows.

## Common Sense & Behavior

### 📋 Roleplay Guidelines

- FearRP: FearRP is not enforced, but you should still value your life reasonably.
- No Suicide RP: You may not commit suicide to avoid roleplay scenarios such as muggings.
- Job Description: You must follow your job description (Medics heal, Merchants/Dealers sell, and Cops protect).
- Merchant Deny: Merchants may not deny service for non-roleplay reasons (for example, refusing to sell guns to a potential raider).

### 🤝 Roleplay Conduct

- No Job Change: You may not change jobs during active roleplay.
- Demotions: Valid reasons include: not doing job, 30+ min AFK, police corruption
- No Vigilante: Do not punish rulebreakers yourself through AOS, KOS, or prop blocking.
- Scamming: Merchants may not scam other players.

## Building Rules

### 🏠 Base Layout & Fairness

- Base Reachable: Bases must remain reachable and accessible at all times.
- Base Entrance: Bases must have exactly one entrance.; Unused map doors must be blocked off.
- Base Jump/Crouch: Raiders must never be forced to crouch or jump inside, outside, or to gain access to a base.
- Base Mazes: Mazes are not allowed.; A maze is more than one 180° turn, more than two 90° turns, or multiple disorienting pathways used to artificially extend raid duration.
- Raid Hallways & Airlocks: Artificial raid hallways and airlocks start at your KOS sign and must not exceed 25 total 1×1 props (1000 units), excluding natural map layouts.
- Base Shooting: Raiders must be able to clearly see you and shoot back.; You may not use tiny hitboxes for an unfair advantage.
- Base Crowbar: Bases must be crowbar-raidable.; Code-only bases are not allowed.
- Base Damage: Bases may not damage players.
- Base Movement: Bases may not slow down or impede player movement.
- Base Entrances: Entrances must be reasonably easy to find, visible, and distinct from surrounding walls, with a minimum 2×2 standing area (80×80 units).
- Base Walkways: Walkways must be at least one 1×1 prop wide (40 units), including ramps.
- Base No Collide: No-collide props must not confuse raiders and should be visually distinct.
- Entity Ladders: Bases must not require entity ladders to access at any time.

### 🚪 Fading Doors

- Fading Door Limit: You may use a maximum of two fading doors to access your raidables.
- Utility Fading Doors: Utility fading doors are allowed (examples: one-way exits, peeking holes, merchant airlocks).; Airlocks/fading door entrances must be identifiable and distinct through color or material.; Merchant or Dealer "airlock" doors are permitted, provided they are publicly accessible and are not part of a base's main entrance or raidable area (2 Max).; Merchants include: Gun Dealer, Medic, Cook, Drug Dealers.

### 🔌 Prop & Wire

- Spawn Build: You may not build in spawn.; Prop climbing, flying, and blocking are not allowed.
- Prop Blocking: Prop blocking and prop spamming are not allowed.
- Wire Abuse: Wire abuse is not allowed.; This includes auto-stealing money, loud sounds, stealing shipments, and using wire to annoy other players.
- Property Respect: You may not place props on, build into, or occupy another player's property or base without permission.

### 🗺️ Placement & Map Rules

- Map Boundary: You may not base outside the map; the rock boundary is the limit.
- Public Space: Do not take up excessive public space.
- Public Building: Only Hobos, Merchants, and Police may build in public.
- Checkpoints: Checkpoints must not block spawn-area entrances or exits, and must not extend raid duration.; All checkpoints must leave another way around that does not require payment to reach the destination.; Only two checkpoints are allowed on the entire map at a time.; There may only be one police checkpoint and one hobo checkpoint or toll booth at a time.; This limit is intended to encourage Hobos and Police to roleplay together at checkpoints.
- PD Building: Non-government players may not build in the PD.; Only two fading doors are allowed inside of the PD.; Being behind the doors of the police department is AOS by default.; No law is required to enforce this.
- Blocking Off: Do not block weed drop-offs, ATMs, trash cans, or the recycler.
- Drop-Offs: Weed drop-off areas must be fully walkable.

### 🚧 Special Building Restrictions

- Doors: Only buy doors you intend to use.
- Skybases: Skybases and excessive aerial builds are not allowed.
- Drop-Off Connections: You may have only one connection to a drug drop-off location.
- Decorative Builds: Decorative aerial builds are allowed.
- Blackout Bases: Blackout bases are not allowed.
- Building Sign: You may not raid a base that has a building sign placed.; You may not place a building sign with valuables in your base.

## Job Specific Rules

### 👷 Civilian

- Gun Dealer: Gun Dealers must intend to sell weapons.; They may not base with another Gun Dealer.; They may defend a criminal base.; They must sell individual weapons, not only shipments.
- Medic: Only one Medic is allowed per raid party.; Only one Medic is allowed per base.
- Theatre Manager: The Theatre Manager must base in the Theatre.
- Hobo: Hitting players with excrement can lead to a valid kill or arrest.
- Hobo Checkpoints & Toll Booths: Hobos may create a checkpoint or toll booth.; Hobo checkpoint and toll booth fees may not exceed $50.

### 🕶️ Criminal

- Hitman: Hitmen may not metagame hits.; Only Hitmen may perform hits.; A valid roleplay reason is not required unless the target is the Mayor.; Hitmen may only raid active target locations.; Only one Hitman is allowed per raid.

### 👮 Mayor & Police

- Mayor: The Mayor must base in the PD.; Gun licenses may include a fee, but the Mayor is not obligated to provide them to criminals.; The Mayor may build outside the PD only for government or roleplay use, such as checkpoints or toll booths.; Major law changes must be announced before enforcement.
- Mayor Grace Period: For 10 minutes after a Mayor is elected, the Mayor may not be raided, mugged, or killed.
- Police: Police must base in the PD and allow all government members to base and roleplay with them.; Follow the command hierarchy.; Attempt to arrest before killing, unless the suspect has a weapon drawn.
- Police Checkpoints: Police may create a checkpoint in public.; Police do not use toll booths, as their checkpoints cannot require a fee.; A police checkpoint may only enforce that a search is required to pass.
- Government Raid: Government players cannot raid with criminals, except alongside a Hitman.
- Job Arrest: Government players cannot raid or arrest someone solely because of their job.
- Laws: Laws must be reasonable and must not contradict server rules.; You cannot make things said in text/voice chat illegal (i.e. Police Disrespect).; AOS laws are allowed; KOS laws are not.; Laws may not target specific individuals or jobs.
- Arrests: Arrests may only be made against lawbreakers.; You may not arrest innocent players, even if bribed.
- Lockdowns: Lockdowns may only be used outdoors.; You may arrest during a lockdown, but KOS is not allowed.; A valid reason is required, such as a bank raid or active shooting.
- Corruption: Corruption is allowed in roleplay, but not against other government members.; Bribes are allowed.; Helping criminals raid the PD or killing government members is forbidden.
- Warrants: Warrants require valid roleplay evidence.; You must witness illegal activity.; Metagaming is not allowed.; Warrants expire on death, jail, or successful raid defense.
- Searches: Searches require a roleplay reason, such as nearby gunshots or loitering near a drug drop-off.

## Raiding & Mugging

### ⚔️ Raiding Rules

- Raiding Guidelines: A raid starts when a prybar is out, a base member is damaged, or when a weapon is drawn on the property.; A raid ends when no raiders remain inside or on the property.
- Mid-Raid: Props may not be moved, changed, deleted, or added during a raid.; You may use an entity ladder to enter a flawed or open base.
- Police Raids: A police raid ends when all attending police die.; Returning to a police raid after death breaks NLR.
- No Counter Raiding: Counter raiding is not allowed.; You may not defend someone else's base unless you are actively basing with them.

### 💰 Mugging Rules

- $ Limit: The maximum mug amount is $1,000.; You must type a mug warning, and the victim must be given 10 seconds to respond.
- Cooldown: There is a 5-minute cooldown between mugs.; Don't mug the same person repeatedly.
- Defense: Victims of a mugging may always defend themselves without warning.
- Shipments: You may mug shipments or guns if you see someone collect them.; The same warning rules apply.

## Minging & Trolling

- The following is not allowed:: Baiting RDM or RDA; Excessive trolling; Preventing others from building; Preventing new players from learning; Repeatedly raiding someone with no valuables; Disobeying staff or reasonable requests

- Targeting: You may not spam-mug, spam-hit, or spam-raid the same person after successful attempts. Space things out and RP with other people or it could be considered harassment.
- Cooldowns: The following timers apply between repeated actions; Mugging: 5 Minute Cooldown; Hits: 15 minutes (Same Player); Raiding (Same Base): 10 minutes; PD Raid: 10 minutes; Mayor Grace Period: 10 minutes (After election); No raiding for 10 minutes after a server crash

### 📝 Reporting Rules

- Reporting: When submitting a report; Be respectful; Do not spam reports; Provide proof (Medal, OBS, or Steam)
- Report Lying: Lying to staff will result in a permanent ban.
- Report Use: Use @ or /Staff in-game to report issues.; EVIPs may jail players in severe cases.
