## Basic RP Rules

### 🔫 RDM / RDA

- RDM Definition: Random Deathmatch (RDM) is killing someone without a valid roleplay reason; RDM is not allowed
- RDA Definition: Random Death Arrest (RDA) is arresting someone without a valid roleplay reason; RDA is not allowed
- RDM Reasoning Rules: Disrespect or threats alone are not valid reasons to kill someone; Taking damage or having items stolen are valid reasons
- Warning Rules: You may kill someone after warning them three times in chat to step away or to leave your property
- Killing the Mayor Rules: Killing the Mayor requires a valid roleplay reason, such as a Police Department raid or mugging

### ⚠️ KOS

- KOS Line Definition: An indicator that implies that moving past a certain point results in being killed
- KOS Line Placement Rules: KOS zones must start at a base's purchasable front door, fading door, or the start of an airlock; KOS lines must be a text sign that implies crossing it results in death
- KOS Clarity Rules: KOS zones must be easy to understand and must never be deceptive
- KOS Boundaries Rules: KOS lines are markers for where KOS begins at a base; Once a KOS line is placed, the intended base behind that line is considered KOS; Example: If a KOS line is at a front door and you enter through a window, you may still be killed
- KOS General Rules: Crossing a clearly marked KOS line is not considered RDM

### 💀 NLR

- NLR Definition: The New Life Rule (NLR) means you may remember past events, but you cannot act on them; You must adhere to NLR on our server
- NLR Trigger Rules: NLR applies on death, job change, and jail release (unless you escaped)
- NLR Raid Rules: You may not return to a raid after death; You must wait until the raid is completed to return to your base as a defender
- NLR Revive Rules: Revived players may continue their raid or scenario
- NLR Hitman Rules: A failed hit cannot be re-attempted; A hit fails upon death

### 🎭 Fail RP

- Fail RP Definition: Fail RP is roleplay that breaks character, violates the setting's logic, or disregards server rules, resulting in poor-quality, unrealistic, or disruptive play; Fail RP is not allowed
- Fail RP Examples: Stealing your base mate's valuables and then starting a new base; Mugging someone with a partner, then killing that partner; Door camping or blocking doors

### 🔍 Metagaming

- Metagaming Definition: Metagaming is using out-of-character (OOC) knowledge to influence your character's in-game decisions; It occurs when your character acts on information they realistically should not know, bridging the gap between what you know and what your character knows; Metagaming is not allowed

## Basic RP Guidelines

- FearRP Rules: FearRP is not enforced, but you should still value your life reasonably
- SuicideRP Rules: You may not commit suicide to avoid roleplay scenarios such as muggings
- Job Description Rules: You must follow your job description (Medics heal, Merchants/Dealers sell, and Cops protect)
- Merchant Rules: Merchants may not deny service for non-roleplay reasons (for example, refusing to sell guns to a potential raider); Merchants may not scam other players
- Job Change Rules: You may not change jobs during active roleplay
- Demotion Rules: Valid reasons include: not doing job, 30+ min AFK, police corruption
- Vigilante Rules: Do not punish rulebreakers yourself through AOS, KOS, or prop blocking

## Building Rules

### 📐 Building Guidelines

- Door Rules: If you buy doors, you must actively use the space behind them as part of your base; You may not purchase interior doors and leave that area unused while basing on the roof or elsewhere
- Base Size Limits: A base's length and width must not exceed 1500×1500 units, measured with the Ruler tool; A base must not be over 1000 units tall from its floor, measured with the Ruler tool
- Rooftop Base Rules: Rooftop bases are built on top of an existing map structure; this is not considered a skybase; Rooftop bases are allowed; You must have a ramp or other clear method of getting to the first fading door; Rooftop-only bases do not need to own the interior doors below; Rooftop base's first fading door must be located on the roof level; Rooftop bases must not obstruct or interfere with property on lower floors; If you do not own the property below, you may not hide or make it difficult for others to access its doors
- Sky Base Rules: A skybase is a base over 750 units tall measured with the Ruler tool from the ground of the map to the top of the base; Skybases are not allowed
- Blackout Base Rules: Blackout bases are not allowed
- Hobo Aerial Build Rules: Hobos may build aerial structures for roleplay features such as ramps and slides

### 🗺️ Placement & Map Rules

- Map Boundary Rules: You may not base outside the map; the rock boundary is the limit
- Public Space Rules: Do not take up excessive public space
- Checkpoint Rules: Checkpoints must not block spawn-area entrances or exits, and must not extend raid duration; All checkpoints must leave another way around that does not require payment to reach the destination; Only two checkpoints are allowed on the entire map at a time; There may only be one police checkpoint and one hobo checkpoint or toll booth at a time; This limit is intended to encourage Hobos and Police to roleplay at their own respective checkpoints; Trying to directly bypass a Hobo checkpoint can result in death; Trying to directly bypass a Police checkpoint can result in AOS
- Police Department Building Rules: Non-government players may not build in the Police Department, including the lobby, without the Mayor's permission; Only two fading doors are allowed inside of the Police Department
- Blocking Off Rules: Do not block ATMs, drop-offs, trash cans, or the recycler
- ATM Rules: Props must not touch or obstruct an ATM; ATMs must be exposed on all sides and be fully walkable; You cannot access an ATM from your base
- Drop-Off Rules: Do not block weed drop-offs; Weed drop-off areas must be fully walkable; Your base may have only one connection to a drug drop-off location

### 🏠 Base Layout & Fairness

- Base Reachability Rules: Bases must remain reachable and accessible at all times
- Base Entrance Rules: Bases must have exactly one entrance; Unused map doors must be blocked off; You must own every door that is part of your base; You may only own doors in areas where you are actively basing
- Entrance Visibility Rules: Entrances must be reasonably easy to find, visible, and distinct from surrounding walls, with a minimum 2×2 standing area (80×80 units)
- Base Walkway Rules: Walkways must be at least one 1×1 prop wide (40 units), including ramps; You may not build aerial walkways from roof to roof
- Entity Ladder Rules: Bases must not require entity ladders to access at any time; Entity ladders may be used to get over public obstacles such as a toll booth or the open roof of a base
- Base Jump/Crouch Rules: Raiders must never be forced to crouch or jump inside, outside, or to gain access to a base
- Base Maze Rules: Mazes are not allowed; A maze is more than one 180° turn, more than two 90° turns, or multiple disorienting pathways used to artificially extend raid duration
- Raid Hallways & Airlock Rules: Artificial raid hallways and airlocks start at your KOS sign and must not exceed 25 total 1×1 props (1000 units), excluding natural map layouts
- Base Shooting Rules: Raiders must be able to clearly see you and shoot back; You may not use tiny hitboxes for an unfair advantage
- Base Crowbar Rules: Bases must be crowbar-raidable; Code-only bases are not allowed
- Base Damage Rules: Bases may not damage players
- Base Movement Rules: Bases may not slow down or impede player movement
- Base No-Collide Rules: No-collide props must not confuse raiders and should be visually distinct

### 🚪 Fading Doors

- Fading Door Limit: You may use a maximum of two fading doors to access your raidables
- Utility Fading Door Rules: Utility fading doors are allowed (examples: one-way exits, peeking holes, merchant airlocks); Airlocks/fading door entrances must be identifiable and distinct through color or material; Merchant or Dealer "airlock" doors are permitted, provided they are publicly accessible and are not part of a base's main entrance or raidable area (2 Max); Merchants include: Gun Dealer, Medic, Cook

### 🔌 Prop & Wire

- Spawn Build Rules: You may not build in spawn; Prop climbing, flying, and blocking are not allowed
- Prop Blocking Rules: Prop blocking and prop spamming are not allowed; You may not spam props in the street or other public areas
- Wire Abuse Rules: Wire abuse is not allowed; This includes auto-stealing money, loud sounds, stealing shipments, and using wire to annoy other players
- Property Respect Rules: You may not place props on, build into, or occupy another player's property or base without permission

## Job Rules

### 👮 Mayor & Police

- Mayor Rules: The Mayor must base in the Police Department; Gun licenses may include a fee, but the Mayor is not obligated to provide them to criminals; The Mayor may build outside the Police Department only for government or roleplay use, such as checkpoints or toll booths; Major law changes must be announced before enforcement; Laws must be reasonable and must not contradict server rules; You cannot make things said in text/voice chat illegal (i.e. Police Disrespect); AOS laws are allowed; KOS laws are not; Laws may not target specific individuals or jobs; Lockdowns may only be enforced outdoors; A valid reason is required for a lockdown, such as a bank raid or active shooting
- Police Rules: Police must base in the Police Department and allow all government members to base and roleplay with them; Follow the command hierarchy: Mayor>Police Chief>Police Officers; Attempt to arrest before killing, unless the suspect has a weapon drawn or you are defending an active raid; Police may create a checkpoint in public; Police do not use toll booths, as their checkpoints cannot require a fee; A police checkpoint may only enforce that a search is required to pass; Searches require a roleplay reason, such as being a police checkpoint, being near gunshots, or loitering near a drug drop-off; Government may raid alongside a Hitman on an active hit; Government may not raid with other criminals
- Police & Raid Rules: While a Police Department raid is active, Government players may respawn and return to defend it; this does not count as breaking NLR; Police responding to an active raid to defend a base are not considered counter raiding; Police may kill active raiders to defend someone's base during an active raid
- Police Department — Lobby & KOS Rules: The Police Department lobby is the front room from the main entrance (the area past the front doors), including the public speaking window and ATM; Entering or being in the PD lobby is never AOS or KOS, and the lobby cannot be made AOS or KOS; KOS cannot start in the lobby; Only areas behind government-owned interior doors are AOS by default; The Mayor or Chief of Police may place a valid KOS line at any Police Department entrance except the lobby; areas beyond that line may be KOS; No law is required to enforce Police Department trespassing as AOS
- Arrest & Warrant Rules: Government players cannot raid or arrest someone solely because of their job; Arrests may only be made against lawbreakers; You may not arrest innocent players, even if bribed; You may arrest during a lockdown, but KOS is not allowed; Warrants require valid roleplay evidence; You must witness illegal activity; you cannot arrest or warrant based off of sound; Warrants expire when the target is jailed or dies; Warrants expire on successful raid defense, including when all attending police die; An individual officer's death does not expire the warrant for other attending police; You may be killed by the person you are trying to arrest or their basemates
- Police Corruption Rules: Corruption is allowed in roleplay, but not against other government members; Bribes are allowed; Cannot raid with criminals, except alongside a Hitman on an active hit; Cannot allow the Police Department to be raided; Cannot kill government officials or allow them to be killed

### 👷 Civilian

- Gun Dealer Rules: Gun Dealers must intend to sell weapons; They may not base with another Gun Dealer; They must sell individual weapons, not only shipments
- Medic Rules: Medics must actively provide service; Only one Medic is allowed per raid party; Only one Medic is allowed per base
- Theatre Manager Rules: The Theatre Manager must base in the Theatre
- Hobo Rules: Hobos may create a checkpoint or toll booth; Hobo checkpoint and toll booth fees may not exceed $50; Hitting players with excrement can lead to a valid kill or arrest

### 🕶️ Criminal

- Hitman Rules: Only Hitmen may perform hits; Hitmen may not metagame hits; Hitmen may only raid active target locations; A valid roleplay reason is not required unless the target is the Mayor; Only one Hitman is allowed per raid

## Raiding, Mugging, & Cooldowns

### ⚔️ Raiding Rules

- Raiding Guidelines: A raid starts when a prybar is out, a base member is damaged, or when a weapon is drawn on the property; A raid ends when no raiders remain inside or on the property
- Building Sign Rules: You may not raid a base that has a building sign placed; You may not place a building sign with valuables in your base
- Mid-Raid Rules: Props may not be moved, changed, deleted, or added during a raid; During a raid, you may use an entity ladder to enter a flawed or open base as allowed under Entity Ladders
- Warrant Raid Rules: A police raid with a warrant ends when all attending police die; Returning to a location with an active warrant after death breaks NLR
- Counter Raiding Rules: Counter raiding is joining a raid while not being a part of the original group; this may also be called third-party raiding; Counter raiding is not allowed
- Base Defense Rules: To defend a base, you must own valuables inside that base (such as your printers or weed stations); You may not defend someone else's base unless you are actively basing with them

### 💰 Mugging Rules

- Money Limit Rules: The maximum mug amount is $1,000; You must type a mug warning, and the victim must be given 10 seconds to respond
- Defense Rules: Victims of a mugging may always defend themselves without warning
- Base Mugging Rules: You may not mug people from your base
- Shipment Rules: You may mug shipments or guns if you see someone collect them; The same warning rules apply
- Cooldown Rules: There is a 5-minute cooldown between mugs; Don't mug the same person repeatedly

### ⏳ Cooldowns

- Targeting Rules: You may not spam-mug, spam-hit, or spam-raid the same person after successful attempts. Space things out and RP with other people or it could be considered harassment; Three or more repeated actions on the same person is considered spam
- Cooldowns: Mugging: 5 Minute Cooldown; Hits: 15 minutes (Same Player); Raiding (Same Base): 10 minutes; Police Department Raid: 10 minutes (Serverwide); No raiding for 10 minutes after a server crash
- Mayor Grace Period Rules: For 10 minutes after a Mayor is elected, the Mayor may not be raided, mugged, or killed

## Minging & Trolling

### 🎭 Minging & Trolling

- Minging & Trolling Rules: Baiting RDM or RDA is not allowed; Excessive trolling is not allowed; Preventing others from building is not allowed; Preventing new players from learning is not allowed; Repeatedly raiding someone with no valuables is not allowed; Disobeying staff or reasonable requests is not allowed

### 📝 Reporting

- Reporting Rules: Be respectful when submitting a report; Do not spam reports; Provide proof with your reports (Medal, OBS, or Steam); Use @ or /Staff in-game to report issues
